// FITTING TOOL

Theorycraft on the go. Upload when it's right.

Build a fit, see capacitor, tank, damage and resources update as you slot modules — calculated by a full dogma engine with your pilot's real skills. Your fits sync across devices with iCloud; in-game fittings load straight from your character.

  • 01Live stats for every module, drone and charge
  • 02Fleets: several ships, one pilot each, boosts included
  • 03Import from the game, save to iCloud, push back to EVE
